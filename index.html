Multi-Domain Modular ML Health Framework

A modular machine learning architecture designed to eliminate health fragmentation. The framework consists of five domain-optimized modules evaluating dietary habits, acute/chronic disease symptoms, 12-lead ECG signals, physical fitness metrics, and integrated health risk stratification.
